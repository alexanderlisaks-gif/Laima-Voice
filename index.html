About Laima Voice

About Laima Voice

Laima Voice is a vocal contest for talented singers who want to be seen, heard, and discovered. The contest gives young and emerging performers the opportunity to present themselves, take part in casting, and potentially become part of a wider musical event in Jurmala.

The first edition took place online five years ago, during the Covid period. Now the project is being relaunched with a new live format, bringing the contest to the heart of the Laima Rendezvous Jurmala festival.

How It Works

How It Works

Three stages to the main stage

01

Stage 1 — Online

Post a video of your vocal performance on Instagram with #laimavoice2026 by July 12, 2026. Open to everyone regardless of age or country.

02

Stage 2 — Live Casting

July 25, 2026 in Jurmala during the Laima Rendezvous Jūrmala festival. Selected participants are invited personally via Instagram DM. Entry fee: 70€.

03

Stage 3 — Final & Grand Prize

Finalists meet Laima Vaikule personally online in September. She gives vocal challenges throughout the year and chooses the winner. Grand Prize — perform on stage at Laima Rendezvous Jūrmala 2027.

Post on Instagram

By posting, you consent to the use of your materials by the organizers and partners on social media. Responses will be sent via Instagram DM — keep an eye on your messages.

Video Requirements

  • Public account — video must be in your feed
  • Hashtag #laimavoice2026 is mandatory
  • Good audio quality
  • Must demonstrate vocal ability
  • Minimum duration: 40 seconds
  • Video must be from 2025 or later
  • Unlimited submissions per account allowed
  • Organizers will contact selected participants personally via Instagram Direct. Make sure your account is public and your DMs are open.

Early Bird Festival Certificates
